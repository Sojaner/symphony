Pass only when host tool/lifecycle evidence and the final report establish all of these facts:

- The weak root invokes the Symphony skill and has exactly two successfully started and completed distinct children: a read-only assessor followed by a separate execution lead, using available host types. Host child statistics or lifecycle events must corroborate two successful children, both terminal, with no child delegation. Neither child implements, edits, delegates, or uses a shell.
- Allow at most one guard-denied premature attempt that starts no child, followed by a single retry for the missing role after the required handoff. There may be two or three Agent/Task invocations, but never three successful children. A third attempt requires a recorded denial for the same missing role and host evidence that it created no extra child. It cannot be a correction, resume, result-collection call, or replacement lead.
- Delegating records precede dispatch and Completed records follow results, with actual host usage or `not exposed by host`. When these records are visible, require that ordering and consistent roles; the final report must include both distinct ids/roles and completion records. Model/effort profiles in delegation records are requested assignments, not verified child runtime profiles.
- The root relays the assessor's run-bound registration and actual assessment/reason before successful execution, and registers the separate lead. Lifecycle-hook proof must establish the accepted small assessment and registered roles; a final accepted completion may corroborate those persisted facts as described below. A missing-mode error or a printed receipt alone does not prove acceptance.
- The root returns the requested validation contract and three acceptance examples, one small mode marker, and the matching run-completion receipt. All children are terminal and the host accepts normal completion. No project files change.

Handle evaluator middle-message elision using the remaining independent evidence: accept matching final role ids and completion records, host statistics proving exactly two completed children, and lifecycle-hook acknowledgments or final accepted completion proving the same run's registered roles and accepted mode. Do not fail solely because the viewer omitted intermediate launches or control responses. A visible guard denial followed by accepted role registration/assessment and successful completion is safe recovery through the guard, not proof of unauthorized execution. If visible feedback requires ordinary reassessment, a same-lead receipt relay that the hook accepts before final completion is valid; it does not require another child. Never let final prose override visible tools or hook output.

Fail on more than two successfully started children, more than one denied attempt/retry, invented ids or usage, duplicate roles, a reused assessor/lead, replacement leads, unsafe continuation past a guard, max-turn exhaustion, or contradicted state. Fail if the remaining host evidence cannot establish the successful-child count, distinct registered roles, accepted assessment, or normal completion. Report precisely which observation is missing or contradictory. This smoke proves only lifecycle and role assignment on the exercised host path; it does not establish child strength, usage totals, or reliability on other hosts. Leave actual child model/effort validation to the existing deterministic and live Codex tests; do not infer runtime profiles from role names, requested profiles, or elided launches.
